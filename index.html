How it works

One phone call a day. One clear update.

01 — Daily

Brigid calls.

A short, warm phone check-in every morning — no apps, no buttons. Brigid asks about pain, sleep, mood, medications, and how the day is going, in plain conversation.

02 — Clinical

Listens clinically.

Behind the friendly voice, Brigid draws on validated geriatric tools — ADLs, IADLs, NYHA, mMRC, FAST — to track recovery across body and mind, and to catch functional decline early.

03 — You

Your family sees everything.

Each call becomes a clear entry in your dashboard. Green when all is well, amber when something needs a closer look, red when it's time to act.

Real-time triage

Every call ends in one of three colours.

Brigid turns a conversation into a clear signal your family — and the care team — can act on instantly. No jargon, no guessing.

Green · Stable

All is well.

Recovery is on track. The day's answers are within healthy ranges. Your family gets a reassuring note and nothing more is needed.

Amber · Follow-up

Worth a closer look.

Something shifted — a missed medication, a rough night, a lower mood. Brigid flags it early so a small thing stays small.

Red · Urgent

Time to act.

A warning sign that needs attention now. Brigid escalates to family and the care team so the right call happens fast.
