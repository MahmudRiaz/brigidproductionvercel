Market

A large, growing, underserved need.

Canada's population is aging fast, and the infrastructure for recovery at home hasn't kept pace. Two buyers feel it acutely: hospital systems carrying readmission costs, and retirement homes carrying duty of care.

  • ~780 licensed retirement homes in Ontario, housing 60,000+ residents
  • 1 in 4 Canadians over 65 by 2030 — a fast-expanding sector
  • 20% of surgical patients readmitted within 30 days post-surgery

Sources: RHRA / Ontario.ca, 2024 · Statistics Canada, 2024 · CIHI, 2022

The road to commercialization

From one hospital to a national standard.

Phase 1 · Pilot

Single hospital / retirement home

  • Launch a 6-month Ontario-based pilot
  • Demonstrate reduced 30-day readmissions vs. matched historical controls
  • Validate clinical outcomes, user experience, and billing feasibility
Phase 2 · Expansion

Across Ontario

  • Partner with additional Ontario hospital systems to scale usage
  • Refine Brigid's AI on real interactions — detection, speech analysis, risk prediction
  • Expand clinical validation and integration across settings
Phase 3 · Rollout

Nationwide

  • Deploy across Canadian hospital systems, adapting to provincial billing & EHRs
  • Support diverse aging populations with multilingual, low-literacy design
  • Establish Brigid as the standard of care for post-surgical recovery
